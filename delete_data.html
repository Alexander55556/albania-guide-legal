Delete My Data — Albania Travel Guide

Albania Travel Guide (developed by Alexander Nalyvaiko) does not require an account or login, and does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or location history.

What data is collected

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app performance and fix crashes. This generates:

  • An anonymous device/install identifier
  • Basic app interaction events (e.g. screens viewed, buttons tapped)
  • Crash and diagnostic logs

If you make an in-app purchase, purchase records are processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to validate and manage your purchase.

How to request deletion

Since the app has no accounts, there is no personal profile tied to your identity to delete. If you would like your anonymous device analytics data removed, email us and we will remove the associated identifier from our analytics records:

Email: albaniaapp.dev@gmail.com
Please include the approximate date you used the app and your device type (e.g. "Android, used around October 2026") so we can locate the relevant records.

What is kept

Purchase records may be retained as required by Google Play and applicable tax/accounting law, even after an analytics deletion request, since these are not linked to your identity beyond what Google Play itself manages.

Retention period

Analytics and crash data not covered by a deletion request is retained according to Firebase's standard retention settings (up to 14 months) and then automatically deleted.
