Delete My Data — Albania Travel Guide

Albania Travel Guide (developed by Alexander Nalyvaiko) has no accounts and no login. We do not collect names, email addresses or precise location.

Data on your device

Most data stays on your device: favorites, planned routes, settings and saved photos. To remove it, delete the app.

Data sent from the app

Anonymous app and device identifiers are used for analytics, crash reporting and purchases. Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics receive:

  • An anonymous app or device identifier
  • Basic app interaction events (e.g. screens viewed, buttons tapped)
  • Approximate location from your IP address
  • Crash and diagnostic logs

If you make an in-app purchase, purchase records are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and by RevenueCat, to validate and manage your purchase.

How to request deletion

Deleting the app does not remove anonymous data that was already sent. To ask us to remove it, or for any other question about your data, email us:

Email: albaniaapp.dev@gmail.com
Please include the approximate date you used the app and your device type (e.g. "iPhone, used around October 2026"). Because there are no accounts, we may not be able to match anonymous records to you.

What is kept

Purchase records may be retained as required by the Apple App Store, Google Play and applicable tax/accounting law, even after a deletion request. Your purchase stays with your store account, so you can restore it if you reinstall the app.

Retention period

Analytics and crash data not covered by a deletion request is retained according to Firebase's standard retention settings (up to 14 months) and then automatically deleted.
