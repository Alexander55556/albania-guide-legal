Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 Oct 2026

Albania Travel Guide respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.

Location

The app requests access to your device's location (fine/precise location) when you tap the "my location" button on the map, so it can show your position and help center the map near you. You will see your device's standard permission prompt at that point, and you can allow or deny it — the app will still work without granting this permission, you just won't see your position on the map. Location data is used only on your device in real time and is never stored, transmitted, or shared with us.

Usage data

The app may use analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics) to understand general usage patterns — for example, which features are used — to help us improve the app. This data is anonymized and not linked to your identity.

Crash and diagnostic data

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically detect and report crashes and technical errors. This helps us identify and fix bugs. Crash reports may include technical details such as device model, operating system version, and the app state at the time of the crash. This data is used solely for diagnosing and improving app stability, and is not linked to your personal identity.

Payments and Purchases

If you choose to unlock additional content, your in-app purchase is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card or billing details — these are handled by Google.

To validate your purchase and keep your unlocked content active across sessions and devices, we use RevenueCat, a subscription and purchase management service. RevenueCat receives purchase-related information from Google Play (such as an anonymous purchase/transaction token and the product purchased) in order to confirm your purchase and sync your unlocked content. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment card details. You can learn more about RevenueCat's own privacy practices at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps — to display the map, place markers, and directions. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudinary — to host and deliver place photos.
  • Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics) — for anonymized usage insights and crash reporting. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — to process in-app purchases.
  • RevenueCat — to validate purchases and manage unlocked content. See revenuecat.com/privacy

Data Storage

Favorites and route selections are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us. Your unlocked-content status is synced via RevenueCat so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or switch devices (see "Payments and Purchases" above).

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
