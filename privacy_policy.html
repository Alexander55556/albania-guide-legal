Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 Oct 2026

Albania Tourist Guide respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers.

Location

The app requests access to your device's location (fine/precise location) when you tap the "my location" button on the map, so it can show your position and help center the map near you. You will see your device's standard permission prompt at that point, and you can allow or deny it — the app will still work without granting this permission, you just won't see your position on the map. Location data is used only on your device in real time and is never stored, transmitted, or shared with us.

Usage data

The app may use analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics) to understand general usage patterns — for example, which features are used — to help us improve the app. This data is anonymized and not linked to your identity.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps — to display the map, place markers, and directions. See Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudinary — to host and deliver place photos.

Data Storage

Favorites and route selections are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us
